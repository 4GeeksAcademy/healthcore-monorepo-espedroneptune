
AI Appointment Assistant

I would create an AI Appointment Assistant that helps patients manage their appointments without having to call the clinic.

Patients could use it to book, confirm, cancel, or reschedule an appointment. The AI would also send reminders by text, email, or app notification before the appointment.

The AI could identify patients who may be more likely to miss an appointment and send them extra reminders or ask them to confirm.

If someone cancels, the AI could offer that open appointment to another patient who is waiting.

This would make booking easier for patients, reduce missed appointments, and help the clinic avoid losing money from empty appointment slots.
